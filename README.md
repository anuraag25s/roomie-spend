# 🛒 Roommate Grocery Expense Tracker

A grocery-focused expense tracking app built from a **product manager’s lens**, optimized for shared households and international students.

---

## 🧩 The Problem

Living with roommates—especially as international students—means managing groceries together:
- Who bought what?
- Who uses which items?
- How do you fairly split shared vs personal groceries?
- How do you handle receipts, tax, and online orders?

Popular tools like Splitwise are **general-purpose** and break down when applied to groceries.  
They don’t understand:
- Grocery categories
- Partial usage
- Receipt-based logging
- Tax distribution
- Online vs in-store purchases

This led to confusion, friction, and unfair splits.

---

## 💡 The Solution

A **purpose-built grocery expense tracker** that:
- Is designed specifically for groceries
- Supports shared households
- Uses receipts as the primary input
- Minimizes manual entry
- Optimizes for fairness and clarity

Built using **AI-assisted development, no-code tools, and rapid iteration**, with a focus on real-world usability over technical perfection.

---

## 👥 Who This Is For

- International students in the U.S.
- Indian households abroad
- Shared apartments (2–6 people)
- Anyone splitting groceries regularly

---

## ✨ Core Features

- ✅ Multi-household support
- 🧾 Receipt upload (in-store & online)
- 📸 Multiple screenshot uploads (for long digital receipts)
- 🧠 Auto-categorization of grocery items
- 🧮 Equal tax distribution across items
- 👤 Per-item split assignment (shared vs personal)
- 📊 Monthly household spend overview

---

## 🧠 Product Philosophy

- Grocery-only focus (no feature bloat)
- Receipt-first UX over manual entry
- Clear ownership and accountability
- Optimize for **real usage**, not edge cases
- Ship → learn → iterate
